# Incident Response Quick Reference

1. Establish incident commander, scope, time zone and decision log.
2. Prioritize safety and business impact. Contain only with an approved plan.
3. Preserve volatile evidence before shutdown when operationally appropriate.
4. Record host identity, user, timestamps, processes, network connections, persistence indicators and relevant logs.
5. Maintain chain of custody: source, collector, timestamp, hash, transfer and storage location.
6. Determine initial access, affected assets, lateral movement, persistence and data exposure.
7. Eradicate validated root causes, rotate exposed credentials and restore from trusted sources.
8. Monitor for recurrence and document lessons learned.
9. Do not interpret absence of telemetry as proof that an event did not occur.
